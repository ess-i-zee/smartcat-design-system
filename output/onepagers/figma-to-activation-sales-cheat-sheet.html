Figma-to-Activation — Sales Cheat Sheet

Internal only

The pitch

Smartcat automates the gap between global design and local execution. We take approved content from Figma, generate localized and market-adapted versions, apply brand and compliance rules, route only necessary work to human reviewers, and deliver approved content into every downstream system and channel.

ROI goes far beyond translation — agency spend, web production, launch speed, compliance risk, and team capacity.

What to listen for — strong signals

  • Figma is the global source; local teams or agencies rebuild from it

  • Copy/paste into the CMS; Jira tickets, spreadsheets, or Slack manage the handoff

  • Markets adapt the global creative (claims, offers, pricing) — not just translate it

  • Small copy changes trigger full re-review cycles

  • No good way to know which local versions are outdated when the global source changes

The key signal

“Once the design is finished, someone still has to manually turn it into all the versions we need.”

Discovery questions that open the door

  • How does a finished Figma design become a live page or experience? Who touches it?

  • How long from approved design to live in every market?

  • Is translation done before or after the page is built? Do markets adapt the creative?

  • What must legal / compliance / brand approve before publishing?

  • What happens when the global source changes after local versions exist?

  • What does the agency charge for production? How many hours per market version?

Also strong

“The design is done, but it takes another 1–2 weeks to get the page live.”

Qualification — a strong opportunity has at least 4 of 6

  • 1

    Figma is the global source of truth for creative

  • 2

    Manual build or handoff happens after design

  • 3

    Operates across many markets (10+ markets/languages)

  • 4

    Local teams adapt the global content

  • 5

    Compliance or approval required before publishing

  • 6

    Content delivered into multiple systems or channels

Best-fit accounts: global creative team on Figma · CMS/commerce downstream (AEM, Sitecore, Contentful, Salesforce…) · high launch volume · agency or internal production rebuilding assets · regulated or claims-heavy content · existing Smartcat localization customers with manual work before or after translation.

Build the ROI on five pillars

Production

Fewer manual builds, lower agency cost, less copy/paste

Localization

Less PM overhead, better reuse, more AI automation

Speed

Faster launch cycles and market activation

Risk

Fewer compliance errors, better governance & audit trail

Capacity

More campaigns and markets on the same team

The question every rep should ask

“Once the global creative is approved in Figma, what work still has to happen before every market can actually use it?”

Then keep asking: ‘Which parts of that process are still manual?’ — that’s where the opportunity is.
