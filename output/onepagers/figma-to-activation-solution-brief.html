Solution brief

Your global creative is approved — but weeks of manual rebuilding, localization, and review still stand between the design and your markets. Smartcat automates the gap between global design and local execution.

The problem

Enterprise teams create more content than ever, but moving it from global design into approved, market-ready experiences is still slow, manual, and fragmented. Every handoff adds copy/paste, agency production costs, duplicated work by market, and lost context between design, content, translation, compliance, and publishing.

The bottleneck is no longer creating the global asset. It’s activating that asset consistently across every market and channel — and keeping every local variant in sync when the global source changes.

The typical workflow today

Figma → handoff → manual production → localization → compliance review → rebuild → CMS & channel publishing

Complexity multiplies fast: products × markets × languages × channels × content changes × reviewers
