Solution brief

Turn Global Content Into Market-Ready Experiences

Creating the global source is only the beginning. Smartcat is the Global-to-Local Content Activation Layer — automating the translation, adaptation, validation, review, and delivery that turn centrally created content into approved, locally relevant, channel-ready experiences in every market.

The enterprise challenge

Between global creation and local execution sits a hidden operational layer: deciding what applies locally, what needs adaptation rather than translation, which claims and disclaimers differ, who must approve, which channels need their own version — and what happens when the global source changes later. Today that work is spread across teams, agencies, spreadsheets, email, and disconnected systems.

The bottleneck is no longer creating or translating content. It’s activating global content consistently across every market and channel.

Complexity grows multiplicatively

Products × markets × languages × channels × content changes × reviewers

At enterprise scale, even a simple launch creates hundreds or thousands of downstream variants and handoffs — slow launches, high production costs, compliance risk, and outdated local content.

The Smartcat activation layer

Global source

Campaigns, product launches, websites, sales decks, training, documentation, packaging, creative assets, product information, regulatory content

Understand → Translate → Adapt → Validate → Comply → Review → Synchronize → Route → Publish

Four problems Smartcat solves

1

Market adaptation

Determine what stays global and what changes locally — claims, offers, pricing, imagery, terminology, disclaimers — then automate the adaptation.

2

Governance

Encode brand rules, approved claims, regulatory requirements, and approval steps. Central governance plus automated local execution.

3

Change propagation

When the global source changes, instantly see which local assets, markets, reviewers, and channels are affected — no full re-review.

4

Channel activation

Orchestrate delivery into CMS, DAM, PIM, CRM, LMS, e-commerce, packaging, and partner portals — where content is actually used.
