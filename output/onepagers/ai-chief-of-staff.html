What Does the AI Chief of Staff Do?

One place to direct all your work

Tell it what you need, or hand it a task. Your AI Chief of Staff determines which coworker — or coworkers — should act, and coordinates them behind the scenes. You never have to learn the org chart of coworkers or track which one handles what.

It gets better every time you use it

You’re not starting from scratch every session. Your AI Chief of Staff learns your role and preferences as you work together, so each session builds on the last. The time that used to go into re-explaining yourself goes straight into the work.

Put it to work today

There’s no separate setup step. Tell it what you need, or drop in a file, and it sets up the workspace itself — shortening the distance from “I have a task” to “the right coworker is on it.”
