# Client logos

Real Smartcat customer logos for any format that shows social proof — a
one-pager logo strip, a deck logo wall, a web `customer-logos` row, a social
asset. Use these instead of placeholder or Smartcat wordmarks.

- **Format:** SVG, single-colour black (`#000` / `#04010E`), no embedded
  images. Most sit on a 68px-tall artboard, a few on 34px; all are cropped
  tight to the mark, so size them by height and let the width follow.
- **Colour:** they are already monochrome, per the design system's
  "Monochrome customer logo" rule. On a light surface use them as they are
  (the components soften them with opacity); on a dark or brand-purple
  surface invert them to white (`filter: invert(1)` or a white-fill copy).
- **File names:** lowercase kebab-case, `&` and `+` spelled `and`
  (`johnson-and-johnson.svg`, `smith-and-nephew.svg`). Stanley Black & Decker
  has two lockups: `stanley-black-and-decker.svg` (horizontal, the default)
  and `-stacked.svg`.
- **Source:** copied from `Marketing/01 Brand/01 Brand Assets/06 Clients/01
  Client logos/Subset/02 Vector` (2026-09-22). Add new logos there first,
  then copy them here with the same naming.
