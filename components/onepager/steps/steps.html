AI Reviewer Agents: How They Work

1

Content Enters the Workflow

Translated content routes to your Reviewer Agent immediately. Analysis begins against your quality standards, terminology, and consistency standards.

2

AI Reviewer Agent Flags What Matters

The agent evaluates translation accuracy, terminology, fluency, and tone, surfacing only the issues that require expert judgment.

3

Experts Review with Focus

SMEs validate high-impact items. Their edits and corrections are captured permanently, feeding back into the agent and improving every future review.

Review cycles shorten. Expertise scales.
