Tips for using the editor

  • Review, correct, and confirm specific segments.
  • View the translation source — see whether a segment was translated by AI or pulled from translation memory.

Confirming and navigating segments

  • Confirm a segment — click the green checkmark or press Ctrl+Enter

Good to know

  • AI-translated segments are automatically saved to translation memory once confirmed.

Before you submit the file

  • All segments are confirmed.
  • Tags and placeholders are intact.
